How to Play

You survived a shipwreck. To be rescued, complete 8 listening missions.

  • Listen to the full audio before answering; choices unlock when playback finishes.
  • Correct answers restore resources and raise your signal.
  • Wrong answers cost resources. If Water, Food or Energy reaches 0%, the adventure ends.
  • Mission 8 contains one question from every previous listening category.
  • Replays are for practice and do not change survival resources.

Audio & accent. Speech is generated on your device. Switch between 🇺🇸 US and 🇬🇧 UK. If your browser has no suitable speech engine, the game tells you instead of silently failing.

Music. The theme loads only when you turn it on. It automatically ducks while speech is playing.

Keyboard. 1–4 answer, P/R play/replay, H hint, Esc closes help.
