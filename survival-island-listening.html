How to Play

You survived a shipwreck. To be rescued, complete 8 listening missions. Each mission trains a specific TOEIC listening trap your ear has to learn to catch.

  • Press ▶ Play to hear the audio. Use Replay as often as you need.
  • Choose the word, number or sentence you hear.
  • Correct answers restore resources and raise your signal.
  • Wrong answers cost resources. If Water, Food or Energy hits 0%, you don't survive.
  • Finish all 8 missions to send the rescue signal and win.

Audio & accent. Speech is generated on your device. Switch between 🇺🇸 US and 🇬🇧 UK accents any time.

Music. The button (top-right) toggles the theme and is remembered. Music automatically drops to 10% while a question plays so you can focus on the listening.

Keyboard. Tab to move, Enter/Space to activate, 1–4 to pick an answer, P to play audio, R to replay.
