How to Play

  1. Choose an unlocked planet. Each one trains one family of modal verbs.
  2. Each attempt contains 12 questions selected from a 14-question bank.
  3. The correct positions are balanced: exactly 3 A, 3 B, 3 C and 3 D per attempt.
  4. Correct: +10 points, +1 crystal and a small Fuel/Oxygen recovery. Every third correct answer in a streak gives +5.
  5. Wrong: −1 life, −28% Fuel and −26% Oxygen. Fuel or Oxygen can now genuinely end a mission before all lives are gone.
  6. A Hint costs 2 points from the current attempt only.
  7. Leaving for the map pauses the attempt. Continue restores the exact question, answers and resources.
  8. Replaying a completed planet can improve its best score but cannot farm the permanent Galaxy score.
