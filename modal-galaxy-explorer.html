How to Play

  1. 1Choose a planet on the Galaxy Map. Each planet teaches one family of English modal verbs.
  2. 2Answer 12 TOEIC-style questions per planet by selecting the best modal verb.
  3. 3Correct answers restore fuel and earn star crystals, points, and streaks.
  4. 4Wrong answers reduce your fuel, oxygen, and lives — so think before you tap.
  5. 5Use hints carefully: each hint costs 2 points.
  6. 6Complete a planet to earn its badge and unlock the next one.
  7. 7Save the galaxy by mastering all eight modal families.
