How it works

You're a corporate investigator. For each case you:

  1. Listen to office conversations and announcements (your device reads them aloud — male & female voices).
  2. Answer questions about what you heard. The transcript stays locked until you've listened.
  3. Collect clues — each correct key answer pins evidence to the board.
  4. Deduce the solution, earn badges, and climb the investigator ranks.

Tip: use keys 1–4 to answer, and the Contrast button for a high-contrast view. Everything is saved automatically.
