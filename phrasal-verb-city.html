How to play

Save the city, one district at a time

  • The city has 10 districts. Each contains 10 useful multi-word verbs used in workplace and everyday English.
  • Each district contains 10 exercises with balanced A/B/C/D answer positions.
  • +10 per correct answer and +5 for every 3 correct answers in a row.
  • A hint costs −2 points from the current attempt only.
  • Replaying a district can improve its record, but cannot inflate your city score.
  • Complete all 10 districts to save the city and unlock the optional Challenge mode with 3 lives.
  • Your current question and progress are saved automatically on this device.
