How to play

Save the city, one district at a time

  • The city has 10 districts. Each teaches 10 useful phrasal verbs for the TOEIC.
  • Answer the exercises. +10 per correct answer, and a +5 bonus for every 3 correct in a row.
  • Stuck? Use the Hint button to see the meaning (it costs −2 points).
  • Finish a district to earn its badge and unlock the next one.
  • Complete all 10 to save the city, then try the optional Challenge mode (3 lives!).
  • Your progress is saved automatically on this device.
