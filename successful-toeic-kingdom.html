How to Play

The realm is in chaos and only a sharp reader can restore order. Travel the kingdom, pass 8 trials, try the bonus Walnut Express, and reach the Successful TOEIC Palace.

Every trial trains a real TOEIC reading skill:

  • Scanning — find names, dates, prices and times fast.
  • Prices & schedules — read brochures, notices and tables.
  • Speed reading — find labelled information before the timer expires.
  • Search under pressure — locate facts in longer documents.
  • Inference — identify what a text suggests, implies or most likely means.
  • Multi-document reading — combine information from two documents before answering.

Stars. Pass a trial for ⭐. Pass within the target reading time for ⭐⭐. A perfect run earns ⭐⭐⭐.

Saved automatically. Progress, stars, score, music preference and your chosen name stay in your browser using LocalStorage.

Questions and documents are generated fresh each run, while answer positions are kept balanced across A, B, C and D.
