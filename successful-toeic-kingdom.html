How to Play

The realm is in chaos and only a sharp reader can restore order. Travel the kingdom, pass 8 trials, brave the bonus Walnut Express, and reach the Successful TOEIC Palace.

Every trial trains a real TOEIC reading skill:

  • Scanning — find names, dates, prices, times fast (Crimson Knight, Bridge of Success).
  • Prices & schedules — read brochures and notices (British Castle).
  • Speed reading — beat the timer (Knights Who Say TOEIC).
  • Search under pressure — find facts in long documents before the badger catches you (Badger Cave).
  • Inference — what a text suggests / implies / most likely means (Magician Trial).
  • Multi-document — cross-check several papers, avoid hasty conclusions (Castle of Wooooow).

Stars. Each trial gives up to ⭐⭐⭐ — one for passing, two for passing quickly, three for a perfect run.

Saved automatically. Progress, stars, score, music and your name live in your browser (LocalStorage). The 📜 Passport (top-left) shows your status any time.

Every visit is different: documents, emails, prices and schedules are generated fresh each time.
