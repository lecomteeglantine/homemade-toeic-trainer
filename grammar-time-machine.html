How to play

Repair the timeline

Five eras of work are damaged: 1850, 1995, TODAY, 2035, 2050. Each era tests a grammar point.

  • Answer the grammar questions. Each correct answer earns +10 points (more with combos).
  • Watch the Time Stability bar: wrong answers make it drop. If it hits zero, the timeline collapses.
  • Finish an era to earn its badge and unlock the next era.
  • Restore all five eras to win. Your progress is saved automatically on this device.
