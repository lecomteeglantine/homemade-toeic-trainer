How to play

Repair the timeline

  • Each era contains 10 questions selected from a larger bank.
  • Correct answers earn points and combos; wrong answers reduce Time Stability.
  • If Time Stability reaches zero, retry the era. Your exact place is saved while you play.
  • Replaying an era can improve its best score, but cannot inflate the permanent score by farming.
  • A, B, C and D are balanced in every 10-question session, but never follow a predictable cycle.
